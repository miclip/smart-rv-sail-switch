# Safety Information

## Critical Safety Warning

**READ THIS ENTIRE DOCUMENT BEFORE PROCEEDING**

The Smart RV Sail Switch modifies a factory-installed safety system in your RV furnace. The sail switch exists to prevent furnace operation when inadequate airflow could cause:

- **Carbon monoxide (CO) buildup** - potentially fatal
- **Incomplete combustion** - fire hazard
- **Heat exchanger damage** - expensive repair
- **Flame rollout** - immediate danger

## Understanding the Risks

### What Could Go Wrong

Even with this project's multiple fail-safes and hybrid design:

1. **Sensor Failure**: If the pressure sensor fails or becomes disconnected, the system may not detect lack of airflow
2. **Wiring Errors**: Incorrect installation could bypass safety features permanently
3. **Code Bugs**: Software errors could cause improper operation
4. **Blocked Ducts**: System may not detect partial blockages that reduce airflow below safe levels
5. **Component Failure**: Relay contacts could weld closed, creating permanent bypass

### This Project Does NOT Protect Against

- Blocked air intake or exhaust vents
- Failed limit switches (overheating protection)
- Gas leaks
- Improper furnace maintenance
- Degraded heat exchanger
- Inadequate combustion air at extreme altitudes

## Required Safety Measures

### Before Installation

- [ ] **Read all documentation** completely before starting
- [ ] **Understand your furnace model** - verify compatibility
- [ ] **Have furnace inspected** by qualified RV technician
- [ ] **Test original sail switch** - ensure it's working at lower altitudes
- [ ] **Check heat exchanger** for cracks or damage
- [ ] **Verify proper venting** - intake and exhaust clear

### During Installation

- [ ] **Disconnect ALL power sources** before working on furnace
- [ ] **Disconnect propane** or turn off gas supply
- [ ] **Take clear photos** of original wiring before any changes
- [ ] **Use proper wire gauges** and connections per documentation
- [ ] **Secure all connections** - vibration-resistant in RV environment
- [ ] **Install in weatherproof enclosure** to protect electronics
- [ ] **Add manual bypass switch** for emergency override

### After Installation - REQUIRED TESTING

- [ ] **Bench test first** - validate circuit operation before RV install
- [ ] **Test at low altitude** (below 5,000ft) where original sail works
- [ ] **Monitor first 10 heat cycles** with CO detector in RV
- [ ] **Verify proper ignition** - no delayed ignition or flame issues
- [ ] **Check exhaust** - should be clear, no soot buildup
- [ ] **Test emergency bypass** switch operation
- [ ] **Document LED indicators** during normal operation for future reference

### Ongoing Monitoring

- [ ] **Install CO detectors** - minimum one near furnace, one in sleeping area
- [ ] **Test CO detectors monthly**
- [ ] **Inspect connections** before each camping season
- [ ] **Check sensor tubing** - ensure not kinked, disconnected, or blocked
- [ ] **Monitor furnace performance** - any changes in operation require inspection
- [ ] **Keep manual bypass accessible** for emergencies

## When NOT to Use This Project

**Do not install this modification if:**

- You are not comfortable working with electronics and 12V systems
- Your furnace has any existing problems or damage
- You cannot properly test the system before relying on it
- You do not have CO detectors or cannot monitor the system
- Your RV will be used by others who don't understand the modification
- Local codes or RV manufacturer warranty prohibit modifications
- You have any doubts about your ability to safely implement this

## Emergency Procedures

### If You Smell Propane

1. **Immediately evacuate** the RV
2. **Turn off propane** at tank
3. **Do not use** electrical switches or devices
4. **Ventilate** - open windows and doors from outside
5. **Call emergency services** if leak is significant
6. **Have system inspected** by professional before re-use

### If CO Detector Alarms

1. **Immediately evacuate** the RV
2. **Turn off furnace** and propane
3. **Ventilate** thoroughly
4. **Seek medical attention** if anyone has symptoms (headache, dizziness, nausea)
5. **Do not re-enter** until RV is fully ventilated
6. **Have furnace inspected** - do not use until cleared by professional

### If Furnace Malfunctions

1. **Turn off furnace** immediately
2. **Use manual bypass switch** to restore original sail-only operation
3. **Do not attempt repairs** while furnace is energized
4. **Document the issue** (LED status, behavior, conditions)
5. **Seek professional help** if problem persists
